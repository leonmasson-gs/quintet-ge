Quintet

Five reels, three free spins a day. Match three or more symbols to multiply your points.

Free to play. 18+

The odds

Each reel is an independent draw. These figures are worked out from the same weights the game uses.

How it works. Every symbol on the five reels scores its points. Three or more of the same symbol multiplies the total. Points build your weekly tier, and tiers reset each Monday.

Demo tools

For reviewing the look. Demo spins are not counted and ignore the daily limit.

Your collection

The rim around a symbol shows how rare it is: plain for common, silver, amber, and a glowing amber for the rarest.

Reel Room

You are placed in a room of 20 players who finished last week on the same tier. No friends needed. Rooms form again every Monday, and your tier never drops during the week.

Community vault

Every spin from every player fills the vault. At 5,000 spins in a day, tomorrow's reels switch to the Floodlight look for everyone. It is a cosmetic unlock only, and it never adds spins.
